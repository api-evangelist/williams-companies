The Williams Companies is an American Fortune 500 energy company focused on natural gas infrastructure, processing, and transportation. Williams owns and operates midstream energy assets including the Transco pipeline (the largest natural gas pipeline in the United States), gathering and processing systems, and storage facilities. Williams serves natural gas producers, local distribution companies, and large-volume end users. The company provides customer portal access for operational data and FERC-regulated electronic bulletin boards for capacity transactions, but does not offer public developer REST APIs.

## Features

| Name | Description |
|------|-------------|
| Transco Pipeline | The Transco pipeline is the largest natural gas pipeline system in the US, running from the Gulf Coast to New York City. |
| Gathering and Processing | Williams gathers and processes natural gas from producing basins in the Appalachian, Gulf Coast, and Rockies regions. |
| Storage Facilities | Williams operates natural gas storage facilities providing seasonal and peak supply balancing for customers. |
| Customer Data Portal | Customer portal providing access to operational data, nominations, and account management for gas shippers. |
| FERC Electronic Bulletin Board | FERC-mandated electronic bulletin boards for capacity release, scheduling, and tariff information on interstate pipelines. |
| OATI Integration | Open Access Technology International (OATI) platform integration for natural gas transportation nominations and scheduling. |

## Use Cases

| Name | Description |
|------|-------------|
| Natural Gas Transportation | End-to-end transportation of natural gas from producing regions to local distribution companies and end users. |
| Capacity Management | Shippers access Williams' FERC electronic bulletin boards to reserve, release, and manage interstate pipeline capacity. |
| Gas Processing | Natural gas liquids extraction and conditioning services for producers in Williams' gathering and processing footprint. |
| Supply Reliability | Utilities use Williams' pipeline and storage assets to balance seasonal demand and ensure supply reliability. |
